# Bild-Prompts — Gerichte-Katalog

Alle Prompts folgen dem gleichen Aufbau, damit die 32 Bilder als einheitliche Reihe wirken (Licht, Kamera-Winkel, Hintergrund, Requisiten konstant — nur das Gericht wechselt).

## Stil-Rahmen (identisch für jedes Bild)

```
low three-quarter camera angle at approximately 30 degrees above horizontal — camera placed just above table height and tilted only slightly downward toward the front of the plate, the plate rim clearly visible as a pronounced elongated ellipse, food primarily seen from the side showing full vertical depth and layering of ingredients, only a small portion of the top surface visible — classic food-blog hero shot perspective (NOT top-down, NOT overhead, NOT bird's-eye, NOT flat lay), soft natural daylight, bright neutral background with light marble or light wood texture. Subtle props at the edges including a folded linen napkin and minimalist cutlery. Natural rich colors, subtle styling, clean food blog aesthetic, highly detailed --no top-down, overhead, flat lay, bird's eye view --ar 1:1
```

## Formel

1. **Opener:** `Appetizing food photography of a [DISH]`
2. **Zutaten sichtbar & im Zubereitungszustand** (`seared`, `roasted`, `grilled`, `julienned`, `sprinkled with`, `drizzled with`)
3. **Container** passend zum Stil (light ceramic bowl / shallow plate / cast iron pan / wooden board / paella pan)
4. **Stil-Rahmen** wörtlich angehängt (siehe oben)
